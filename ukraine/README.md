# The war in Ukraine, 2026. Viewing notes

Viewing notes, in French and English, on Lex Fridman Podcast #504 with Michael Kofman and Preston Stewart.

- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2hJkprFnzhY
- Official transcript: https://lexfridman.com/preston-stewart-michael-kofman-transcript

## Disclaimer

These notes summarise what two American analysts who support Ukraine say. They report the guests' view, not that of the author or the author's employer. The episode says almost nothing about civilians or displacement. The figures cited have not been verified. The notes describe the situation in November 2026. They were written with the support of a language model, Claude (Anthropic), from the official transcript and the video. In case of doubt, the video and the transcript are authoritative.

## Licence and credits

- Text: CC BY 4.0 (proposed, to be confirmed by the author before going online).
- Images in `img/`: stills from Lex Fridman Podcast #504, © Lex Fridman. They are reproduced for commentary only, each with its credit and a link to the passage. The text licence does not cover them.
- Fonts: Barlow Condensed, Source Sans 3 and JetBrains Mono, loaded from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).

## Build

```bash
python3 build.py
```

The content, in both languages, lives in `build.py`. Styles are in `style.css`. The script writes `index.html` (a headless fragment, for the private preview) and `docs/index.html` (a standalone page, with `docs/img/`).

## Publishing on GitHub Pages

Nothing is online yet. When the time comes, push the folder to a repository, then pick the `main` branch and the `/docs` folder under Settings > Pages. `docs/index.html` carries a `noindex` tag, to be removed in `build.py` if the page should be indexed.
